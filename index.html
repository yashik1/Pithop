🛣️ Pithop

A free road-trip planner that finds the best stops along your route.

Enter where you're driving from and to, and Pithop maps your route and finds interesting places to stop along the way — scenic viewpoints, quirky roadside attractions, nature and parks, history, museums, and food — filtered by how far off your route they are and how long you'd like to stop. Save your trips, follow them with live in-browser navigation and voice directions, and optionally sign in to share great places with other travellers. It runs in your browser with nothing to download.
