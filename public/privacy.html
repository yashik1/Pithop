🛣️ Privacy & Data Sources

Last updated: July 2026

SideQuest is a route-planning app that finds interesting stops along a driving route. It is built to know as little about you as possible: no account, no sign-up, no advertising trackers, no analytics cookies.

What leaves your device

  • The place names you type (origin, destination, autocomplete text) are sent to a geocoding service to be turned into map coordinates, and those coordinates are sent to a routing service to compute your route.
  • If you tap 📍 “use my location”, your device's GPS coordinates are used as the route origin and sent to the routing service like any other origin. The “only stops ahead of me” feature processes your GPS position entirely on your device — it is never uploaded.
  • Route coordinates are sent to place-data services to find stops along the way.

What stays on your device

Your last trip and your saved-trips library (routes, stops, and the stops you added to your plan) are stored in your browser's local storage so the app works offline. They never leave your device. To erase them, clear this site's browsing data.

Community places you share

If you use “📍 Add a place”, the place you submit — its name, optional note, category, suggested visit time and map coordinates — is stored in our database and shown publicly to every user of the app. Don't include personal information in names or notes. Places reported by several users are hidden automatically. To request removal of a submission, contact us (below).

Signing in: when sign-in is enabled, adding a place requires an account (Google or email/password), handled by Supabase Auth. This keeps contributions accountable. We store your account's user id (for spam-limiting and moderation) and a short display name — your first name and last initial — which is shown publicly as the contributor (“Added by Jane D.”). Your email address is used only for authentication and is never shown to other users or attached to a place. Browsing and reporting never require an account.

Services we rely on

Purpose Service
Maps, geocoding, routing, roadside places Geoapify (when configured), otherwise OpenStreetMap public services (tiles, Nominatim, Photon, OSRM demo, Overpass)
Landmark descriptions & photos Wikipedia / Wikimedia (content under CC BY-SA)
Hosting Vercel (standard web-server request logs)
Community places database Upstash (stores only the submitted place data described above)
Sign-in (to add a place) Supabase Auth (Google / email accounts; used only when enabled)
Navigation links “Open in Google Maps” links simply open Google Maps — nothing is shared until you tap one

Each service receives only what is needed for its job and is governed by its own privacy policy.

Affiliate disclosure

SideQuest may include clearly labeled affiliate links — “🎟️ Book tickets” (Viator or GetYourGuide), “🏨 Hotels” (Booking.com) and “⛽ Gas cash back” (Upside). If you buy something after following one of these links, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. The links simply open the partner's site in a new tab; the app itself never sees or processes any payment, and no purchase data flows back to us.
