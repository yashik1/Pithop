Toronto to Ottawa: worthwhile breaks on the way east

The Toronto–Ottawa drive can be done as a straightforward highway trip or as a slower day with one meaningful stop. Kingston works for a substantial break; smaller waterfront and heritage towns are better when you want to stretch your legs without committing to a full attraction.

Four stop ideas to shape your day

Check each place against your actual route before leaving; detours depend on your starting point, traffic and the road option you choose.

Longer break · food and history

1. Kingston

Kingston is a natural place to break the drive with a meal, a waterfront stroll or a historic downtown wander. If you have limited time, choose one area and park once rather than trying to cover several attractions.

Explore Kingston on Maps ↗
Short walk · riverside

2. Brockville waterfront

Brockville can make a good reset stop for a walk by the St. Lawrence and a coffee or meal. It suits a flexible itinerary: if you are running late, keep it brief; if the day is going well, spend longer exploring downtown.

Find Brockville waterfront ↗
Historic village · slower pace

3. Merrickville

Merrickville is known for its heritage character, canal setting and small-town shops. It is a better fit for travellers who enjoy wandering at their own pace than for anyone trying to minimize every minute off the highway. Confirm what is open on your travel day.

Scenery · seasonal planning

4. Rideau Canal area near Ottawa

Instead of adding a distant attraction, consider saving time for a walk beside the canal once you reach Ottawa. This gives you a scenic finish without making the middle of the drive feel rushed.
